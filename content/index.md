# The Hacker's Cookbook

Welcome to my personal **Hacker's Cookbook** — a collection of pentesting experiences, insights, and practical knowledge. This site is where I document everything I’ve learned from hands-on labs (especially **Hack The Box**) and my professional work in cybersecurity, as well as valuable concepts and techniques gained during my university studies.  

You can learn more about me on the [About](/about) page.

---

## Why a Cookbook?

I’ve designed this site not as a typical blog, but as a **structured cookbook** for ethical hacking and penetration testing. Each "recipe" follows a consistent format, starting with a theoretical overview of a tool, technique, or technology. It then provides actionable strategies, tips, and useful payloads to help you exploit it or apply it effectively in your own pentests.  

Many pages link to related content, allowing you to **navigate seamlessly** between theoretical concepts, practical guides, and **lab walkthroughs** where I demonstrate real-world applications of these techniques. Whether you’re seeking a refresher on a tool or a deeper dive into a specific attack vector, you'll find interconnected content to guide your learning.

---

## A Disclaimer  
This site is not intended to be a scientific or perfectly authoritative reference. Instead, it serves as a **platform for sharing my tips, tricks, and experiences**. While I strive for accuracy, there may be errors or imprecise details. If you notice anything that could be improved, feel free to [contact me](/contact) — I’d love to collaborate and make this platform even better.
